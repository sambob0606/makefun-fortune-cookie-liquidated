A single original fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated, written for make.fun's "Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader" bounty. The program writes one line of at most 20 words to `/output/entry.txt`, where a judge model playing the freshly liquidated trader reads it two at a time against other fortunes and keeps the one that makes them laugh instead of cry.

> The market ate your margin and left you the crumbs; chew them slowly, for crumbs, properly savored, are a feast.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
